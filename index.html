ONE PIECE

One Piece is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It's about the story of a boy named Luffy who sets out to become the Pirate King.

About the Story

Luffy and his crew travel across the Grand Line searching for the legendary treasure called the One Piece.

  • Monkey D. Luffy - The main protagonist who dreams of becoming the Pirate King.
  • Roronoa Zoro - A swordsman who aims to become the world's greatest swordsman.
  • Sanji - A cook who wants to find the All Blue.
